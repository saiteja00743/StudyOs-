How we'll build it together
Instead of trying to finish everything in one go, we'll treat this like a real startup.
∩é╖ Sprint 1: Landing page + Design System
∩é╖ Sprint 2: Authentication + Dashboard
∩é╖ Sprint 3: AI Chat (ChatGPT-style)
∩é╖ Sprint 4: Notes + PDF Intelligence
∩é╖ Sprint 5: Quiz + Flashcards + Planner
∩é╖ Sprint 6: Analytics + Deployment + Launch
This keeps the project manageable, gives you something usable after every sprint, and
produces a strong portfolio piece while staying completely within the free tiers of the tools
you've chosen.
